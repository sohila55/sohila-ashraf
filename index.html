project 01

Wiki E-Commerce

paused — track order phase

the problem

Most solo-built shopping app tutorials stop at product listings. Real e-commerce apps need working auth, live inventory, a persistent cart, and order visibility — the parts that actually make an app usable, not just a portfolio screenshot.

the approach

Built end-to-end in Flutter with Firebase as a real backend — Firestore for product and order data, Firebase Auth for accounts — and Cubit for predictable, testable state across every screen.

what's working

  • Register & login with Firebase Auth
  • Browse products with category filters
  • Full product detail screens
  • Cart — add, remove, manage items
  • Favourites / wishlist
  • User profile screen
